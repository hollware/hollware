The admin is laid out as Linear lays out a workspace, with Nuxt UI's dashboard: a sidebar that collapses and resizes, with the site to work in at the top (and the sites to switch to), search on ⌘K, the sections with their icons (the reviews that wait counted, the collections of the site one click away), and the person at the foot; each page is a panel beside it, its title and its actions in the bar at the top.
