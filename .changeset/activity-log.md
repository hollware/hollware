An activity log of who did what (ADR-037). Every operation that is not a read, every change of tokens, people and invitations in the admin, sign-in and sign-out, an invitation accepted, an app allowed or revoked, and each scheduled publication write a line: who did it as Hollware verified it (a person, a token, an app, or the deployment), what the client declared apart, the action, its target and its outcome, refusals of a scope included. The log is append-only in D1 (migration `0016_audit_log`). Owners read it on the admin's Activity page and download it as CSV.

The owner decides what it keeps, in the site's `audit`: `ipAddresses` is `off` by default (`hashed` keeps an HMAC of each address keyed by the site, never the address), and `retentionDays` is `null` by default (forever), or 30 to 3650 days. `hollware init` and `npm create hollware` take `--ip-addresses` and `--audit-retention-days`.
