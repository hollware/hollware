Nested entries in the admin. A collection's settings turn nesting on and set its levels. The entries of a nested collection show as a tree in a locale: drag one onto another to put it inside, above or below, or move it from its menu with the keyboard (up, down, into the one above, out to its parent), and start a new entry inside it. An entry shows its ancestors in the breadcrumb, moves under another parent in the dialog that changes its URL, and opens on the site at its whole path.
