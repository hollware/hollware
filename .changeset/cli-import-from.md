`hollware import --from nuxt-content | emdash` moves a site from another CMS: the `content/` of a Nuxt Content site (pages routed as Nuxt Content routes them, their Markdown kept, locales from folders with `--locale-dirs`), or an EmDash site (its `.emdash` package or a seed, Portable Text written as Markdown, simple fields as frontmatter, what has no form left out and listed, and `--publish` publishing only what EmDash had published).
