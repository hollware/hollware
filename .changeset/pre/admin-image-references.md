The form of an entry edits more of its frontmatter. A field of the image kind, new in the editor of a collection, gets a button that picks an image of the library and writes its `asset:` reference. A field that a `reference` rule points at another collection picks its entries by title, one or several. A field the form cannot show, such as an object, is named above the form with a button that opens the Markdown, where it is edited.
