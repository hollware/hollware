Responsive images: when an image is uploaded, Hollware also makes its narrower copies, the `media.widths` of the site (640 and 1280 pixels by default, `[]` for none), served at `<url>/<width>`. `upload_asset`, `get_asset` and `list_assets` answer `widths` and `srcset`; the HTML of `get_entry`, and the document a site reads, give each image its `srcset`, `sizes`, `width` and `height`; and a summary read by a site (`get_entry`, `list_entries`, `createHollware()`) carries `images`, the size and `srcset` of each image its frontmatter names, by URL, to lay out a cover. The admin sets the format, quality, widest width and copies in Site, and shows the copies of each image. Images uploaded before keep none.
