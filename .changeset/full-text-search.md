Full-text search (ADR-029). The operation `search` finds the published entries that hold every word of a query, in their title, tags and text, best first, with a snippet that marks the match; it is a tool over MCP, `GET /v1/search` in the read API, and `search` over RPC and in `@hollware/client`. Migration `0008_search.sql` adds the FTS5 index, kept by triggers in the step that publishes or unpublishes, and indexes what is published already. "migracion" finds "migración".
