Publication events (ADR-025): a publish or an unpublish writes its event to an outbox in the same batch, and the Worker delivers it from a Queue (its new `queue` handler) or, without one, from the cron trigger: to the webhooks of the site, signed with HMAC-SHA256 (`signWebhook` and `verifyWebhook` in `@hollware/core`), and to IndexNow. Each subscriber gets each event once; a failure is retried and ends in the dead-letter queue. A site declares its `url` and its `events` in the config.
