`publish` takes `publishAt` to schedule a publication (ADR-025): the entry is checked now and waits as `scheduled`, and the Worker's new `scheduled` handler, run by a cron trigger every minute, publishes it at its time in the name of who scheduled it. A change after scheduling, or `unpublish`, takes it back to a draft. The migration `0003_events_and_schedules.sql` adds the tables of schedules and of the outbox of v0.2.
