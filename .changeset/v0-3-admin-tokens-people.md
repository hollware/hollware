Tokens and people in the admin. An owner signed in to the admin makes, lists and revokes personal tokens (a token is shown once, and gets no scope its maker lacks), and invites editors and authors with a link to `/setup`. These are routes of the admin's own (`/v1/admin/…`), not operations: they take only an access token of the admin's client with `schema:admin`, so neither a personal token nor another MCP client can make a token.
